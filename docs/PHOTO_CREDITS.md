# Product photos

Photos are optional. Without one, a product shows its drawing.

## Add a photo

1. Pick a photo **you are allowed to use**: your own, or a free-licence photo.
2. Check it shows **no brand name or logo** (contest rule).
3. Save it in `src/assets/photos/` named after the product id, as `.jpg`, `.png` or `.webp`.
   Landscape (4:3) works best; keep each file under about 300 KB.
4. Add a row to the table below.
